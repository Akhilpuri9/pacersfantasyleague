How to Play
Pacers Fantasy League — complete rulebook
🏒
Team Selection
  • Pick exactly 11 players from both playing teams
  • Max 7 players from one team
  • Must have at least 1 Wicket Keeper
  • Must have at least 1 Batsman
  • Must have at least 1 All Rounder
  • Must have at least 1 Bowler
  • Select your Captain (2× points) and Vice Captain (1.5× points)
  • Team is locked once the match starts — no changes allowed
👑
Captain & Vice Captain
  • Captain earns 2× their total fantasy points
  • Vice Captain earns 1.5× their total fantasy points
  • Choose wisely — this is the biggest decision you'll make
  • If the Captain doesn't play, the VC automatically gets 2× points
🏆
Season Points
  • After each match, players are ranked by their team's fantasy points
  • 1st place gets 11 season pts
  • 2nd place gets 10 season pts
  • And so on down to 11th place = 1 pt, 12th+ = 0 pts
  • Season points determine the final tournament winner
👥
Groups
  • Create a group and share your 6-character invite code with friends
  • All members of a group compete against each other
  • Group leaderboard tracks points across the full season
  • You can be in multiple groups simultaneously
